Logging in and signing up

Every user account is just a name and a PIN (4 or more digits) that you choose yourself on the sign up page - there's no email, and nobody has to approve you first. Pick any name that isn't already taken and a PIN you'll remember; both are on the honor system, so use a PIN nobody else would guess if you'd rather other people not touch your submissions.

Signing up also means reading and agreeing to the Acceptable Use Agreement shown on that page, and retyping your name to confirm it - both are required before an account is created. The short version: if you couldn't bring it to school, you can't print it here.

Forgot which name you signed up with, or typed your PIN wrong a few times? There's no self-service password reset - ask an admin, who can reset your PIN for you from the Users page. Admin accounts are separate from user accounts and aren't self-signup at all (see the admin log in page) - they're created by whoever runs the server.

Submitting a print

From My submissions, upload a model (.stl, .obj, .3mf, or a .zip containing several of the first three - each model inside becomes its own job, not one combined plate) and it's sliced automatically. A .3mf containing more than one object is the opposite of a zip: they're combined into a single print at the positions already saved in the file, since a multi-part .3mf (hinges, gears, anything meant to be printed together as one piece) needs those parts to stay exactly where they were placed, not split apart into separate jobs. While it's still a draft - before you submit it into the shared queue - you can change any of these as many times as you like, and each change just re-slices the same upload in place:

  • Generate supports and support style - whether the slicer should add supports at all, and if so, which kind (Automatic, Grid, Snug, Organic, or one of the two Tree styles). Leave supports off for anything that doesn't need them; supports use extra filament and take longer to remove.
  • Color - pick a specific filament color, or leave it "Any available" if you don't mind which one, so an admin doesn't have to change filament just for your job. This is best effort: the printer can't report what's actually loaded, so it's only as accurate as an admin's last inventory update, and you can change it later even after submitting (see below).
  • Scale and rotate X/Y/Z - scale keeps the model's proportions; rotation is in degrees on each axis. Both are on the model's edit page, alongside a live 3D preview so you can see the result (and, with supports on, where they'll actually go) before deciding it's ready.

Once it looks right, Submit puts it into the shared queue for an admin to review - it's visible to admins as soon as it's submitted, there's no separate pre-review step before that.

Job statuses, and what each one means

A job moves through these statuses, in this order:

  • Submitted - just uploaded; being sliced right now.
  • Sliced - a draft, still yours to change and hasn't been submitted into the queue yet.
  • Slice failed - the slicer couldn't process it (see the technical detail on the job for why). Still a draft - adjust settings and re-slice, or let it expire.
  • Queued - you've submitted it; waiting for an admin to look at it.
  • Approved - an admin greenlit it. It's still waiting its turn, since only one job can be on the printer at once - approving doesn't start it printing by itself.
  • Rejected - an admin declined it, with a note explaining why. This is terminal; see "Restoring a job" below if you want another attempt at it.
  • Printing - an admin has actually released it to the printer.
  • Done - finished printing successfully.
  • Failed - the print itself failed or was cancelled partway through (not the same as "Slice failed" above, which never reached the printer at all).
  • Expired - a draft (Submitted, Sliced, or Slice failed) that sat too long without ever being submitted, and was automatically cleared out.

Editing, re-slicing, restoring, and reprinting

Color can be changed at any point up through Approved, even after you've submitted - changing it never triggers a re-slice, since color doesn't affect the model itself.

Restore is available on a job that's Rejected, Done, Failed, or Expired: it copies that job's model into a brand new draft with the same settings it had, re-slices it, and takes you straight to that draft's edit page - the quickest way to try again with a rejected job (after fixing whatever the admin's note called out) or to print a past job again with different settings.

Reprint is available only on a Done job, and is the opposite of Restore: it re-queues the exact same already-sliced file with no changes and no re-slicing at all - use it when you just want another one of exactly what you already printed.

Your account settings

From Settings you can change your theme (the sidebar/page look) and its light/dark mode independently of each other, and change your PIN. None of this affects your jobs.
