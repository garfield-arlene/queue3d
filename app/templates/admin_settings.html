Site settings

A model that's been uploaded and sliced but never submitted to the queue is a "draft" - visible only to the user who uploaded it, never to admins. cleanup_drafts.py (wire it into cron - see app/README.md) removes any draft older than this, so abandoned drafts don't sit around forever.

Every timestamp everywhere in the app (activity log, job history, "finished at", etc.) is shown in this timezone - takes effect immediately for everyone, not just after a restart. Data is still stored and compared internally as UTC regardless; this only changes what a viewer actually reads on the page.

A job still awaiting a decision (queued or approved, not yet released) that's been waiting at least this long moves from the normal queue to a separate Old jobs view - out of the way there, not shown in both places at once. Doesn't apply to a job that's already printing.
