A .zip with more than one model becomes one job per model (up to {{ max_zip_models }} models per zip) - not a single combined-plate print. A .3mf with more than one object is the opposite: they're combined into a single print, at the positions already saved in the file - the right behavior for a multi-part assembly (hinges, gears, anything meant to be printed together as one piece), not split into separate jobs the way a zip's files are.
