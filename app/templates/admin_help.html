Admin help

Covers what's specific to running the queue as an admin. For the submitting/statuses/account material every user (including you) also has, see the regular help page.

Running the queue

A submitted job needs an explicit Approve (or a Reject with a note the submitter will see) before it can print. Approving only marks it greenlit and waiting its turn - only Release actually sends an approved job to the printer, and only one job can be on the printer at a time. Once a print finishes on its own, Mark done or Mark failed (with a reason) records the outcome - though a print finishing, failing, or being cancelled is also noticed automatically as a safety net, in case nobody clicks either button in time.

The printer-status banner at the top of the queue page shows whether the printer is currently paired and reachable, and covers pairing a new printer if it isn't yet.

Users, admins, and colors

Users lets you reset a forgotten PIN, or disable/enable/delete an account. Admins manages admin accounts themselves (there's no self-signup for those - see the regular help page's "Logging in and signing up" section). Colors tracks which filament colors are available and roughly how much of each is on hand, which is what populates the color choice users see when submitting.

Site settings, history, and backups

Settings covers site-wide options like how long an unsubmitted draft can sit before it expires, the display timezone, and the age threshold for "old" finished jobs. Finished jobs and Old jobs are history views (old jobs can be bulk-deleted or requeued from there); Activity log is a record of who did what. The dashboard's own "Download support bundle" link produces a .tar.gz of the database, activity log, every submitted feedback report, and any model that ever hit a slicing error or was referenced by a feedback report, for troubleshooting without needing internet access. Backup status (when the last one succeeded, and from which drive) is shown right on the dashboard.

The System page

System shows this Pi's own live CPU, memory, and network activity, refreshing every couple seconds - a per-core CPU breakdown and memory/swap/disk usage at the top, and history graphs of the last few minutes below. Useful for checking whether the Pi itself (not the printer or the network) is the bottleneck during a big multi-model upload or a lot of concurrent slicing, or just for keeping an eye on disk space and CPU temperature over time.

Feedback

Feedback lists every problem report or comment submitted by any user or admin, most recent first, and includes the same form for reporting one yourself. Every submitted report is folded into the support bundle automatically (see above) - there's no separate step needed to include it.
